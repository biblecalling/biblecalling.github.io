  Within our <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/jesus-calling-october-2026/">Jesus Calling October guide</a>, few biblical phrases are richer or more life-giving than Jesus' invitation: 'Abide in Me.' The October 23 reading in *Jesus Calling* unpacks that sacred reality, transforming theological doctrine into an intimate, warm invitation to share in the very joy of Christ.
</p>

## What Is Jesus Calling October 23 About?

Jesus Calling October 23 centers on the mystery of abiding in Christ and experiencing the mutual joy that flows from union with Him. The devotional describes opening our hearts to receive Jesus' loving favor and being filled with His life, until His joy and our joy intertwine. Anchored in John 15:4, John 15:11, and Zephaniah 3:17, the practical takeaway is to rest in Christ's delight over you and stay connected through brief, ongoing prayers.

## Today's Theme: Abiding in the Vine: The Shared Joy of Jesus

<p>
  Scripture invites believers to turn their attention toward Jesus and receive the warm light of His presence. It pictures opening our hearts to receive His smile of grace, allowing His life to intertwine with ours until a profound union is formed: Christ living in us, and us resting in Christ. In that union, joy is no longer something we labor to manufacture; it is the natural byproduct of remaining connected to the true Vine, bringing our hearts before God in <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/prayer/">communion in prayer</a>.
</p>

## Bible Verses Related to Today's Theme

### The Vine and the branches
<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">John 15:4 (KJV)</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abide in the vine; no more can ye, except ye abide in me.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; John 15:4 (KJV)</div>
</div>
<p>As highlighted in our <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/bible/">Bible study articles</a>, a branch does not grunt and strain to produce grapes; it simply remains attached to the vine, drawing life and nutrients from the root. Discipleship is about staying connected to Christ.</p>

### Fullness of joy through abiding
<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">John 15:11 (KJV)</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;These things have I spoken unto you, that my joy might remain in you, and that your joy might be full.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; John 15:11 (KJV)</div>
</div>
<p>Jesus did not give commands to burden us; He gave them so that His divine joy would dwell within us and make our joy complete.</p>

### God rejoicing over His people with singing
<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">Zephaniah 3:17 (KJV)</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;The LORD thy God in the midst of thee is mighty; he will save, he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love, he will joy over thee with singing.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; Zephaniah 3:17 (KJV)</div>
</div>
<p>One of the most astonishing verses in all of Scripture: God is not frowning with perpetual disappointment; He rejoices over His redeemed children with joyous song.</p>

## Why This Matters in Everyday Life

<p>
  Many believers mistakenly imagine God's primary attitude toward them is frustration or weary tolerance. Zephaniah 3:17 paints the exact opposite portrait: the Almighty God resting in His love and singing over His children with joy. When you understand that Christ genuinely delights in fellowship with you, the discipline of prayer stops feeling like a grueling chore and becomes a life-giving sanctuary, which we cultivate through a steady <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/devotionals/">quiet time reflections</a>.
</p>

## Practical Ways to Practice This Today

1. **Receive God's delight over you:** Read Zephaniah 3:17 three times slowly, meditating on the reality that God rejoices over you through Christ.
2. **Stay plugged into the Vine throughout the day:** Utter brief, one-sentence prayers during your daily tasks, maintaining a continuous lifeline of connection.
3. **Pray for unity with a difficult person:** In the spirit of John 17, ask God to bless and give grace to one fellow believer you find hard to get along with.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Jesus Calling October 23 about?
<p>Jesus Calling October 23 centers on the mystery of abiding in Christ and experiencing the mutual joy that flows from union with Him. The devotional describes opening our hearts to receive Jesus' loving favor and being filled with His life, until His joy and our joy intertwine. Anchored in John 15:4, John 15:11, and Zephaniah 3:17, the practical takeaway is to rest in Christ's delight over you and stay connected through brief, ongoing prayers.</p>

### What is the core message of Jesus' vine and branches analogy in John 15?
<p>In John 15:4–11, Jesus explains that disciples bear fruit not by self-effort, but by abiding in Him. Just as branches draw life from the vine, believers experience supernatural vitality and full joy by remaining connected to Christ through faith and prayer.</p>

### Does Jesus Calling claim to be Scripture?
<p>No. Author Sarah Young explicitly stated that Jesus Calling is not Scripture. It is a devotional designed to encourage believers in their walk, while the Holy Bible remains the sole inspired Word of God.</p>

### How can I practice abiding in Christ throughout my day?
<p>Meditate on Zephaniah 3:17 and God's loving delight over you, whisper short prayers during routine tasks to keep your heart connected to the Vine, and pray for a fellow believer you find difficult.</p>
