## Reflection: Counting the Small Mercies

The Bible asks us to give thanks "in" everything, not "for" everything. That difference is gentle. You don't have to pretend a hard thing is good. You are invited to find the mercies that stand beside it.

Think of a night sky in a city. You may see only a handful of stars until you step away from the bright lights, and then thousands appear. Gratitude is stepping away from the glare of worry long enough to see what was already there: a warm meal, a kind message, a working phone line to someone you love.

---

## Try This Today

* **List Five Small Mercies:** Write five small things you're thankful for before bed.
* **Thank Someone by Name:** Thank one person by name, in person or by message.
* **Give Thanks at Meals:** Say a short thank-you to God at each meal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does thanking God mean I must feel happy?
No. Gratitude is a choice to remember God's goodness, even when feelings lag.

### Why does the Bible command thanks so often?
A thankful heart is protected from bitterness and envy, and it keeps our focus on God.
