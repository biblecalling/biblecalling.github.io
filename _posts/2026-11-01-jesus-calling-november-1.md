## Quick Answer: What Is Jesus Calling November 1 About?

This companion reflection for Jesus Calling November 1 looks at time as something God hands us, not something we must beat. In regions of the United States and other parts of the world observing the end of daylight saving time today, clocks turn back one hour—a helpful picture of receiving an "extra" hour with open, unhurried hands.

## Reflection: The Gift of an Unhurried Hour

Most of us treat time like a currency we are constantly short of, racing from one obligation to the next with hurried breaths. Moses prays differently in Psalm 90. He does not plead for more hours; he asks for the grace to number them—to see each day as counted, purposeful, and precious before God. Wisdom, in biblical terms, is not high speed. It is holy attention.

If you live in an area that gains an hour through daylight saving time today, resist the impulse to immediately fill that space with extra tasks. Give the first ten minutes back to God in quietness and allow them to be unproductive on purpose. A rested, quiet heart hears the Shepherd's gentle voice far clearer than a cluttered, exhausted mind. As you plan your upcoming week, consider how cultivating <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/jesus-calling-november-6/">rest for the weary</a> prepares your soul to serve others with patience.

## Try This Today: A Simple Time Practice

1. **Spend ten quiet minutes:** Dedicate the first ten minutes of your morning or quiet hour in stillness with one Scripture verse before checking devices.
2. **Clarify priorities:** Write down three things that truly matter for your spiritual walk and family this week, and intentionally cross out one nonessential task.
3. **Build a "God buffer":** Schedule a ten-minute margin between two appointments tomorrow to breathe, pray, and reset your thoughts before Christ.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the Bible say anything about busyness?

Scripture speaks extensively about rest and wisdom (Psalm 127:2; Mark 6:31). The biblical pattern is not laziness or apathy, but an intentional spiritual rhythm: diligent, faithful work followed by withdrawing into quiet fellowship with Jesus Christ.

### Does everyone experience daylight saving time today?

No. Many countries, as well as select regions within the United States (such as Arizona and Hawaii), do not observe daylight saving time changes. Regardless of whether your local clocks changed, God's invitation to receive time as a sacred gift remains universal for every believer.
