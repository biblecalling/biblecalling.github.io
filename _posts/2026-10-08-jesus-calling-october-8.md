<p class="lead">
  Some mornings you wake up with a vibrant sense of God's closeness. Other mornings your emotions feel flat, dry, or distant. If your confidence in God's love rises and falls with your emotional weather, the October 8 reading in *Jesus Calling* offers a vital, steadying anchor for your faith.
</p>

## What Is Jesus Calling October 8 About?

Jesus Calling October 8 centers on the steady, unchanging nature of God's love compared to our fluctuating emotions. The reading cautions against projecting our shifting moods onto God, reminding believers that Christ's love remains constant regardless of how we feel. Grounded in Jeremiah 31:3, James 1:17, and Romans 8:39, the practical takeaway is to separate subjective feelings from biblical truth and rest in God's eternal covenant love.

## The Core Spiritual Lesson: God's Steadfast Love Beyond Changing Feelings

<p>
  The October 8 reflection addresses a common human vulnerability: we often mistake our emotional fluctuations for changes in God's posture toward us. When we feel motivated and spiritually energized, we imagine God loves us more; when we feel downhearted or weary, we fear He has pulled away. The devotional challenges this misconception by turning our eyes toward Jesus' unchanging character. Circumstances shift constantly, but the cross of Christ settled God's love once and for all.
</p>

## What Scripture Teaches Us

### An everlasting covenant love
<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">Jeremiah 31:3 (KJV)</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; Jeremiah 31:3 (KJV)</div>
</div>
<p>God spoke these tender words to Israel when they were weary and wayward. His love did not start when they performed well, and it did not dissolve when they stumbled. It is an everlasting love that pursues us with mercy.</p>

### A God without shadow or shift
<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">James 1:17 (KJV)</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, and cometh down from the Father of lights, with whom is no variableness, neither shadow of turning.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; James 1:17 (KJV)</div>
</div>
<p>Earthly shadows lengthen and shift as the sun moves across the sky. God, however, possesses no variableness—His character, faithfulness, and loving nature are constant across every season.</p>

### Nothing can sever His bond
<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">Romans 8:38–39 (KJV)</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life... nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; Romans 8:38–39 (KJV)</div>
</div>
<p>The Apostle Paul exhausts every extreme in creation to assure believers that God's grip on us cannot be pried loose by troubles, distress, or personal weakness.</p>

## Navigating Everyday Life With This Truth

<p>
  When we condition God's love on our daily performance or emotional state, spiritual anxiety inevitably follows. We become hyper-focused on analyzing our feelings instead of trusting Christ's finished work. While emotions are authentic parts of the human experience that deserve honest attention, they make terrible foundations for spiritual truth. The stability of our Christian walk rests outside ourselves—in the unchanging character and promises of God.
</p>

## Three Concrete Action Steps for Today

1. **Separate the emotional feeling from the biblical fact:** When you feel spiritually numb or distant today, declare aloud: 'My feelings are fluctuating, but God's love for me has not moved.'
2. **Anchor your thoughts in Jeremiah 31:3:** Write this verse on an index card or phone note, reciting it during a routine pause such as commuting or walking.
3. **Receive grace before taking action:** Before tackling your work or family obligations, spend sixty silent seconds simply resting in the reality that you are unconditionally loved by God.
