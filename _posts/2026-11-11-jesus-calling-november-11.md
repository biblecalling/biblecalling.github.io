## Reflection: Remembering, Honoring and Making Peace

On November 11, communities across the globe observe solemn commemorations—known variously as Veterans Day in the United States, Remembrance Day across Commonwealth nations, or Armistice Day in Europe. While traditions and national observances differ according to local history, the shared human weight of this date calls for reverent pause. 

Scripture never glorifies the destruction and devastation of war. Instead, the Bible honors sacrificial love—the quiet, courageous willingness to place oneself in harm's way so that neighbors and future generations might live in safety and freedom. Jesus spoke the words of John 15:13 on the eve of His own ultimate sacrifice upon the cross, giving His life to redeem a broken humanity.

Remembering faithfully requires more than ceremony; it calls for compassionate action. It calls us to express gratitude to veterans, to care for service members and families bearing the visible and invisible scars of combat, and to heed Jesus' call in the Sermon on the Mount: *"Blessed are the peacemakers."* Peacemaking begins right where we live. It calls us to lay down weapons of bitter gossip, defensive retaliation, and hostile words in our homes, workplaces, and communities. As we also explore in [Setting Down What Someone Else Did](/jesus-calling-november-14/), pursuing reconciliation and release reflects the very heart of the God who reconciled us to Himself.

---

## Try This Today

* **Express Sincere Gratitude:** If you know a military veteran, active service member, or a family who has sacrificed through military service, take a moment to thank them personally with an honest word or written note.
* **Pray for Healing & Peace:** Dedicate time in prayer for those recovering from physical or psychological wounds, for grieving families, and for leaders making decisions that affect human lives in conflict zones around the world.
* **Practice Everyday Peacemaking:** Identify one strained relationship or tense conversation where you have felt defensive. Choose to respond with listening, calm gentleness, and an invitation toward mutual understanding.

## Prayer for Today

Comfort every home that bears the ache of loss, and grant healing to those whose wounds remain unhealed. Forgive us where we harbor bitterness or stir up discord. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is November 11 observed around the world?
Observances vary by region. In the United States, Veterans Day honors all living military veterans who have served honorably. In the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and other nations, Remembrance Day commemorates all military personnel who have fallen in the line of duty, often symbolized by the red remembrance poppy.

### Is biblical peacemaking simply avoiding conflict?
No. Biblical peacemaking is active and principled, not passive retreat. Avoiding difficult issues often allows injustice and resentment to fester. True peacemaking addresses grievances with truth, humility, and grace, striving for righteous reconciliation (Romans 12:18).
