## Quick Answer

This reflection for **Jesus Calling November 21** shows that Jesus Himself invited His friends to rest, and that rest is part of a faithful life, not a distraction from it.

---

## Scripture for Today

* **Mark 6:31 (KJV):**  
  > "And he said unto them, Come ye yourselves apart into a desert place, and rest a while: for there were many coming and going, and they had no leisure so much as to eat."

## Reflection: Permission to Rest on a Saturday

The disciples had just returned from busy ministry, and people were still pressing in. Jesus did not say, "Keep going." He said, "Come apart and rest."

A field that is planted every year without a break slowly loses its strength. A season of rest lets the soil recover. Our minds and bodies are the same. A slower Saturday is not wasted time. It is how you stay faithful for the weeks ahead.

## Try This Today

* **Step Outside Unplugged:** Spend 15 minutes outdoors, phone away.
* **Rest in a Psalm:** Read a Psalm slowly and let it be enough.

## Question to Carry This Week

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is resting a lack of faith?
No. The Bible presents rest as trust that God is at work even when we are not.

### What if I have no time to rest?
Start small. Even ten intentional minutes of quiet can help.
