## Quick Answer

This reflection for **Jesus Calling November 30** closes the month by remembering that God's mercies are fresh every morning and His faithfulness does not run out.

## Scripture for Today

* **Lamentations 3:22-23 (KJV):**  
  > "It is of the LORD'S mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness."

## Reflection: Mercies That Arrive Before You Wake

The writer of Lamentations spoke these words in a season of deep sorrow. He wasn't ignoring the pain. He chose to remember something bigger: God's compassion does not run out.

Think of bread delivered to a doorstep before sunrise. You didn't earn it and you didn't wake up early to fetch it. It was simply there. God's mercies arrive the same way, fresh each morning, whether the last month was easy or hard. As November closes, look back with thanks and forward with trust.
