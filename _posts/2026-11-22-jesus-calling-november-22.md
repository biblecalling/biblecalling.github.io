## Reflection: Jesus Is Lord Over This Whole Week

We usually picture Jesus as gentle, and He is. But the Bible also shows Him as King, and that matters on anxious days. A gentle friend who cannot help is a comfort. A gentle friend who is also Lord of all is a refuge.

Imagine a pilot calmly speaking through turbulence. Passengers settle because they trust the person at the controls. Knowing that Jesus reigns doesn't remove turbulence, but it changes how we sit through it. Today, as you worship, bring the week ahead and place it under His care.

---

## Try This Today

* **Declare His Lordship:** Name one worry and say aloud, "Jesus, You are Lord over this."
* **Sing with Confidence:** Sing or read a hymn that celebrates His rule.
* **Intercede for Leaders:** Pray for one leader or authority in your life.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Christ the King Sunday?
A celebration in many church traditions on the last Sunday before Advent, honoring Christ's reign.

### Does Jesus' kingship feel distant?
The Bible shows a King who also draws near, forgives, and walks with His people.
