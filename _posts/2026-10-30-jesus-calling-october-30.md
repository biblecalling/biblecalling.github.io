## What Is Jesus Calling October 30 About?

Jesus Calling October 30 focuses on becoming quieter and more attentive to God's guidance, describing stillness as the classroom where we learn to hear the Shepherd's voice. The devotional encourages believers to cultivate internal calm that carries into everyday life, while warning against worldly mental clutter. Grounded in John 10:27, Jeremiah 29:13, 1 Kings 19:12, and 1 John 4:1, the practical takeaway is to create quiet pockets for prayer and test every spiritual impression against the Bible.

## Key Takeaway: Quietness as the Classroom: Learning to Hear the Shepherd

<p>
  The reading reminds us of Jesus' promise that His presence is constantly with us. Yet many people miss that reality because their minds are earthbound and cluttered with noise. Christ desires for His sheep to hear His voice continually. Quietness is the classroom where our spiritual hearing is trained; as we practice stillness in <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/prayer/">attentive prayer</a>, we learn to carry that settled peace into the noisy marketplace of daily life.
</p>

<p>The Apostle John gives the indispensable safeguard: every impression, feeling, or teaching must be evaluated against the revealed Word of God.</p>

## Overcoming the Daily Challenge

<p>
  It is essential to understand what 'hearing God' means in biblical orthodoxy. For most believers, hearing God is not an audible sound, but a growing familiarity with His character through Scripture, the convicting whisper of the Holy Spirit, a settled peace, or the confirmation of wise Christian counsel. Crucially, *Jesus Calling* is an inspiring <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/devotionals/">daily devotional walk</a> work, whereas the Holy Bible is inspired, inerrant Scripture. We use devotionals as prompts for prayer, but we test everything against the Bible.
</p>

## Practical Spiritual Disciplines for Today

1. **Carve out a five-minute silent pocket:** Choose a quiet spot with no phone or music, and sit in intentional stillness before God.
2. **Read Scripture before you listen:** Ground your thoughts in a biblical passage first, ensuring your mind is shaped by God's Word before seeking guidance.
3. **Test every impression against the Bible:** Never treat personal feelings or impressions as infallible; measure them against Scripture and confirm with mature believers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Jesus Calling October 30 about?
<p>Jesus Calling October 30 focuses on becoming quieter and more attentive to God's guidance, describing stillness as the classroom where we learn to hear the Shepherd's voice. The devotional encourages believers to cultivate internal calm that carries into everyday life, while warning against worldly mental clutter. Grounded in John 10:27, Jeremiah 29:13, 1 Kings 19:12, and 1 John 4:1, the practical takeaway is to create quiet pockets for prayer and test every spiritual impression against the Bible.</p>

### How does John 10:27 describe hearing the voice of Jesus?
<p>In John 10:27, Jesus says, 'My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me.' Recognizing the Shepherd's voice is the natural mark of a disciple, developed through quiet attentiveness, familiarity with Scripture, and walking in obedience.</p>

### How should believers evaluate spiritual impressions against Scripture?
<p>First John 4:1 instructs Christians to 'try the spirits whether they are of God.' Every impression, feeling, or devotional writing must be measured against the written Word of God. If an impression contradicts biblical Scripture, it must be rejected.</p>

### How can I create space to listen to God today?
<p>Carve out five quiet minutes without digital devices, read a short passage of Scripture before praying, and write down any impressions so you can test them against God's Word and wise counsel.</p>
