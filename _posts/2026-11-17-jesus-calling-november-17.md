## Quick Answer

This reflection for **Jesus Calling November 17** explains that Christ's peace is a gift that stays with you in busy days, not a reward for having a calm schedule.

---

## Scripture for Today

* **John 14:27 (KJV):**  
  > "Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid."

## Reflection: Peace That Doesn't Depend on a Quiet Day

We often say, "I'll feel peaceful once things settle down." But a calm calendar rarely arrives. Jesus spoke these words on the night before His arrest, which was anything but peaceful. The peace He offered was never tied to easy circumstances.

Picture a lighthouse in a storm. The waves do not stop, yet the light stays fixed and keeps guiding ships. Christ's peace works like that light. It doesn't silence the storm around you; it gives your heart something steady to look at while the storm passes.

---

## Try This Today

* **Pause and Receive:** When tension rises, pause and pray, "Jesus, I receive Your peace."
* **Three Slow Breaths:** Take three slow breaths before replying to a stressful message.
* **Surrender Before Sleep:** Write down one thing you can leave in God's hands tonight.

## Prayer for Today

Lord Jesus,  
I don't want a peace that disappears when my day gets loud. Settle my heart with Yours, and help me carry Your calm into the rooms I walk into today.  
*Amen.*

## Question to Carry This Week

Where am I waiting for circumstances to change before I allow myself to be at peace?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it wrong to feel anxious even though I believe in Jesus?
No. Feelings come and go. Jesus' words invite you to bring them to Him, not to hide them.

### How is Christ's peace different from relaxation?
Relaxation depends on the situation. Christ's peace rests on His presence and promises.
