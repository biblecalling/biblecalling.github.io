  Within our <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/jesus-calling-october-2026/">October devotional series</a>, some spiritual truths are so familiar that we stop truly hearing them. 'God is with us' might be the most familiar phrase in the entire Christian vocabulary. The October 25 reading in *Jesus Calling* urges us never to let familiarity numb the breathtaking reality of Christ's perpetual presence.
</p>

## What Is Jesus Calling October 25 About?

Jesus Calling October 25 centers on the perpetual presence of Christ and the deep significance of His biblical names: Jesus ('the Lord saves') and Immanuel ('God with us'). The devotional warns against letting familiarity dull our awe, encouraging readers to stay in continuous communion with Him throughout routine tasks. Supported by Matthew 1:21–23, Psalm 139:7, and 1 Thessalonians 5:17, the practical takeaway is to narrate your day to Christ and meditate on His name.

## Understanding Today's Focus: Immanuel: God With Us in the Mundane and the Sacred

<p>
  The reading reminds believers that Jesus is Immanuel—God with us for all eternity. It cautions against allowing that reality to become so routine that it loses its impact. His constant presence should be an unending wellspring of peace and joy. The meditation encourages us to reflect deeply on the sacred meanings of His names: Jesus, because He saves His people from sin, and Immanuel, because He abides with us forever, bringing our hearts before God in <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/prayer/">heartfelt prayer</a>.
</p>

    &ldquo;And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins... they shall call his name Emmanuel, which being interpreted is, God with us.&rdquo;

<p>As highlighted in our <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/bible/">Scripture encouragement guide</a>, together, these two names summarize the gospel: God rescues us from our sins, and then He stays with us forever.</p>

### An inescapable presence
<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">Psalm 139:7 (KJV)</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy presence?&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; Psalm 139:7 (KJV)</div>
</div>
<p>David marvels that whether he ascends into heaven or dwells in the depths of the sea, God is already there, sustaining him with tender care.</p>

### Praying without ceasing
<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">1 Thessalonians 5:17 (KJV)</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;Pray without ceasing.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; 1 Thessalonians 5:17 (KJV)</div>
</div>
<p>Constant prayer is not about repeating endless phrases; it is an abiding consciousness of God's presence as we move through ordinary duties.</p>

## Living With Perspective & Grace

<p>
  Salvation is not merely a legal ticket to heaven punched in the past, followed by an isolated life until death. Salvation is an invitation into an eternal relationship. When you grasp that Immanuel walks beside you while you do laundry, sit in rush-hour traffic, or manage difficult emails, ordinary tasks become sacred space shared with the King of kings, which we cultivate through a steady <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/devotionals/">daily devotional reading</a>.
</p>

## How to Put This Into Practice Today

1. **Meditate on the name Immanuel for two minutes:** Sit quietly and repeat the phrase: 'Immanuel, God with me,' allowing the wonder of the incarnation to settle in your heart.
2. **Narrate your day to Christ:** Silently share one small thing that delighted you and one thing that irritated you directly with Jesus.
3. **Whisper a transition prayer:** Before moving from one meeting or chore to the next, take a breath and whisper: 'You are with me here too.'
