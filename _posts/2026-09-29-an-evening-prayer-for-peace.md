As twilight settles and the day draws to a close, our minds often become battlegrounds. We replay awkward conversations, worry about tomorrow's deadlines, or dwell on tasks left unfinished. Many nights of sleepless tossing and turning are caused not by physical restlessness, but by an unburdened soul trying to carry the weight of the world into bed.

Sleep is fundamentally an act of faith. Going to sleep requires closing our eyes, surrendering control, and trusting that the universe will continue running without our supervision because God never sleeps.

<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">Peaceful Rest</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;In peace I will both lie down and sleep; for you alone, O Lord, make me dwell in safety.&rdquo;

In Psalm 121:4, we are reminded that *&ldquo;he who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep.&rdquo;* Since God is going to stay awake all night watching over you, there is no need for both of you to stay up.

## An Evening Prayer of Surrender

Dim the lights, turn off your screens, and release the day with this quiet evening prayer:

<div class="prayer-box">
  <div class="prayer-box-title">An Evening Prayer for Restful Sleep</div>
  <p>
    Heavenly Father,
  </p>
  <p>
    The sun has set, the day's work is done, and I come to rest in Your presence. Thank You for sustaining me through every hour of this day—for the breaths I took, the bread I ate, and the unseen protection You provided.
  </p>
  <p>
    Where I stumbled, spoke hastily, or sinned against You today, I ask for Your gentle forgiveness through the blood of Jesus. Wash my conscience clean.
  </p>
  <p>
    Where I left things unfinished, I place them into Your capable hands. The world is Yours, not mine. I surrender my to-do list, my anxieties about tomorrow, and my concerns for the people I love.
  </p>
  <p>
    Grant me deep, restorative, sweet sleep tonight. Post Your holy angels around my home. Quiet my racing thoughts with Your peace, and let me awaken tomorrow refreshed and ready to serve You. In Jesus' loving name, Amen.
  </p>
</div>

## Evening Rhythms for Calming the Mind

1. **Review with Gratitude:** Name three specific blessings from today—no matter how small. Thanking God shifts the brain from anxiety to rest.
2. **Close the Day's Ledger:** Write down any urgent thoughts for tomorrow on a notepad, physically close the book, and say: *&ldquo;That is for tomorrow; tonight belongs to rest.&rdquo;*
3. **Meditate on God's Watchfulness:** As you lay your head on the pillow, repeat Psalm 91:4: *&ldquo;He will cover you with his pinions, and under his wings you will find refuge.&rdquo;*

## Scripture Promises for the Night

* **Proverbs 3:24:** &ldquo;If you lie down, you will not be afraid; when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet.&rdquo;
* **Psalm 127:2:** &ldquo;It is in vain that you rise up early and go late to rest, eating the bread of anxious toil; for he gives to his beloved sleep.&rdquo;
* **Psalm 3:5:** &ldquo;I lay down and slept; I woke again, for the Lord sustained me.&rdquo;

## Rest in His Love

Let go of every striving. The Lord is your keeper, your shade, and your peace. Sleep well tonight in the safety of His hands.
