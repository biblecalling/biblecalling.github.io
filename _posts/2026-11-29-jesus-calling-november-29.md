## Reflection: The First Sunday of Advent and a Hope That Waits

Advent means "coming." For centuries, God's people waited for the promised Savior. Their waiting was not empty; it was full of hope, because they trusted the One who promised.

A single candle in a dark room changes everything. You don't need a bonfire to see the way. Today, as many churches light the first Advent candle, remember that Christ has already come, and He will come again. Your waiting, too, can be full of hope.

## Try This Today

* **Light a Candle:** Light a candle or pause in silence for two minutes.
* **Pray for Hope:** Pray for someone who needs hope this season.
* **Read the Prophecy:** Read Isaiah 9:2-7 slowly.

## Prayer for Today

In Jesus' name,  
*Amen.*

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Advent?
The four-week season before Christmas, focused on preparing for Christ's coming.

### Can I observe Advent at home?
Yes. A candle, a short reading, and a prayer are enough.
