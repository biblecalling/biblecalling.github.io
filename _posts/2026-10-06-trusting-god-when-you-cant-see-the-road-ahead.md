Most of us want a complete plan before we take the next step. But faith rarely works that way. Whether you're facing a career change, a health concern, or a relationship in transition, the question is the same:

**Can I trust God with what I can't see?**

Today's reading from Sarah Young's *Jesus Calling* centers on this idea: we don't know what's around the bend, but the One leading us does, and that is enough.

## Walk by Faith, Not by Sight

The Apostle Paul put it simply:

<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">Scripture Foundation</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;For we walk by faith, not by sight.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; 2 Corinthians 5:7 (KJV)</div>
</div>

Faith isn't denial. It doesn't ask us to ignore real problems. It asks us to trust the Shepherd more than the circumstances.

This is the same Shepherd David describes in Psalm 23:1:

<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">The Shepherd's Care</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; Psalm 23:1 (KJV)</div>
</div>

When the future feels uncertain, faith does not mean pretending everything is predictable. It means remembering that God remains faithful even when our circumstances are unclear. Exploring <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/categories/faith/">biblical faith</a> teaches us that God's presence is constant, even when the path ahead turns into unfamiliar terrain.

## Don't Stop at the Bend in the Road

Proverbs 3:5–6 offers a promise for exactly this season:

<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">Divine Direction</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; Proverbs 3:5&ndash;6 (KJV)</div>
</div>

Notice that the promise is direction, not a full map.

God often reveals the next step without showing us the entire journey. That can be difficult for us because we naturally want certainty. We want to know how everything will turn out before we move forward.

But faith invites us to take the next step with God, even when we cannot see what comes after it. As we learn in our daily <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/categories/devotionals/">devotional reflections</a>, trusting God's timing frees us from the anxious urge to predict every outcome ourselves.

## Receive the Mountaintop Moments

Scripture often connects mountains with encounters with God.

In Matthew 17:1–8, <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/categories/jesus/">Jesus</a> led Peter, James, and John up a high mountain, where they witnessed His glory. Those moments of clarity and closeness are gifts.

Don't rush past them.

Slow down. Pray. Give yourself space to recognize God's presence and allow His peace to settle your heart. Sometimes a season of uncertainty contains moments of unexpected clarity. Treasure those moments, but remember that they are not necessarily the destination.

## Come Back Down and Shine

Peter, James, and John didn't stay on the mountain. Jesus eventually led them back into ordinary life.

Likewise, Matthew 5:16 reminds us:

<div class="scripture-highlight">
  <div class="scripture-highlight-title">Letting Your Light Shine</div>
  <blockquote class="scripture-highlight-text">
    &ldquo;Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.&rdquo;
  </blockquote>
  <div class="scripture-highlight-ref">&mdash; Matthew 5:16 (KJV)</div>
</div>

The peace we receive is meant to be carried into everyday <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/categories/christian-life/">Christian living</a>. That means home, work, relationships, family, and community.

## What This Means Today: Walking by Faith in Daily Life

When we step into our daily routines &mdash; managing emails, cooking meals, navigating difficult conversations &mdash; trusting God stops being an abstract concept and becomes an hour-by-hour practice:

- **Relinquish the Need to Predict Everything:** Acknowledge to God that your limitations are real, but His wisdom is limitless.
- **Take the Next Obedient Step:** When you cannot solve the entire year, solve today with faithfulness and prayer.
- **Reflect Christ's Quiet Calm:** People around you are often carrying unspoken fear. Your calm trust in Jesus can be a steady light that directs others to God's love.

## A Personal Reflection: Trusting God on an Unseen Road

*A Personal Reflection:* There have been seasons when I wanted God to show me exactly where a decision would lead before I took the first step. I wanted certainty, but what I often received was something quieter: enough peace to take the next step and enough faith to leave the rest in God's hands. Looking back, I can see that I didn't need the entire road revealed at once. I needed to trust the One walking with me.
