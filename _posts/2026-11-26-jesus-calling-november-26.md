## Quick Answer

This reflection for **Jesus Calling November 26** makes room for both thankfulness and sorrow. Both can be brought to God on the same day.

---

## Scripture for Today

* **Psalm 136:1 (KJV):**  
  > "O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever."

* **Colossians 3:17 (KJV):**  
  > "And whatsoever ye do in word or deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God and the Father by him."

## Reflection: A Full Table and an Empty Chair

For many, today brings laughter and warm food. For others, there is an empty chair, a strained relationship, or a quiet house. Both experiences are real, and Scripture makes room for both.

The psalm says God's mercy lasts forever. That means gratitude does not require a perfect day. You can thank God for His faithfulness while still missing someone deeply. Like a candle on a dark table, thanksgiving doesn't erase the shadows, but it gives light to see by.

---

## Try This Today

* **Pray at the Table:** Pray before the meal, even if it is brief.
* **Name with Gratitude:** Say the name of someone you miss and thank God for their life.
* **Reach Out Today:** Reach out to someone who might be spending today alone.

---

## Prayer for Today

Lord,  
Thank You for Your steady mercy. Be near those who feel the empty chair today, and bless every table with peace and kindness.  
In Jesus' name,  
*Amen.*
