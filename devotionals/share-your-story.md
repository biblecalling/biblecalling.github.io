<p class="lead">
  Many readers find it encouraging to hear how other people keep a quiet time habit in real life, with busy schedules and hard days. If a devotional has been part of your life, you are welcome to share a short story.
</p>

## What We Are Looking For

A few honest paragraphs (about 60 to 150 words) in your own words. It can be small and ordinary. You do not need a dramatic story.

## Questions That May Help

1. What season of life were you in when you started reading devotionals?
2. What did you find hard about keeping the habit, and what helped?
3. Was there a verse or reading that stayed with you? (A reference such as Psalm 34:18 is enough.)
4. What has changed for you, even in a small way?
5. What would you tell another woman who is just starting?

## How We Handle Your Story

- **Permission first.** We publish nothing without your clear written permission, and we will show you the final text before it goes live.
- **You choose your name.** You can use your first name, an initial, or stay anonymous. We never publish your email, exact location or other private details.
- **Light editing only.** We may shorten or fix spelling, and we will not change your meaning without your approval.
- **Adults only.** Stories must come from people aged 18 or older.
- **No payment, no pressure.** We do not pay for stories or ask anyone to say positive things.
- **Take it back any time.** You can ask us to remove your story at any time.
- **Not advice.** Stories are personal experiences, not medical, legal or professional advice. Please do not include other people's private information.

## How to Send Your Story

Please visit our [contact page](/contact/) and choose **&ldquo;Sharing a Personal Testimony&rdquo;** in the topic dropdown. Tell us a little about your quiet time routine and what has encouraged you. Our team reads every message prayerfully and will reach out to confirm your consent before anything is ever considered for publication.

By sending your story, you confirm that you are 18 or older, that it is your own account, and that you give Bible Calling permission to publish it as described above.
