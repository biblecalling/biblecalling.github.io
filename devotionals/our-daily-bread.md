## What Is Our Daily Bread?

Our Daily Bread is a daily devotional published by Our Daily Bread Ministries. It has been read by many people for decades and is offered free in several forms, so it is a common first devotional for people who want a simple daily habit.

Bible Calling is an independent website. We are not connected with Our Daily Bread Ministries. This page is our own description and guide, written to help readers understand the devotional.

## What a Daily Reading Usually Includes

Formats can change over time, so check the official site for the current layout. In general, a daily reading includes:

- **A Scripture passage.** The reading is built around a section of the Bible.
- **A short article.** A few paragraphs that explain an idea from the passage, often with an everyday example.
- **A prompt to think or pray.** A closing thought or question you can take into your day.

Most readings take only a few minutes, which is why many people use it alongside coffee or at the start of work.

## Where to Read Our Daily Bread Today

The official daily reading is published by Our Daily Bread Ministries at <a href="https://odb.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">odb.org</a>. Depending on what they offer at the time, you may also find it as a mobile app, an email subscription or a printed booklet. Visit the official site to see the current options.

We do not copy or repost their daily articles. That keeps the text with its authors and makes sure you read it exactly as published.

If you want a second reading for the day, or you prefer a quieter, prayer-focused style, you can read our own reflection on [Today's Devotional](/devotionals/today/).

## How to Get More From a Daily Bread Reading

A short article is a starting point, not the whole conversation. Here is a simple way to go deeper:

1. **Open your Bible first.** Read the passage itself before the article, so you hear the Scripture in its own words.
2. **Read the article once, then the verses again.** Notice what changed in how you understand them.
3. **Ask one question.** What does this show me about God, and what is one thing I can do today?
4. **Pray in your own words.** If words are hard to find, use one of our [prayer guides](/prayer/).
5. **Write one sentence.** A short note at the end of the week shows you what has been on your heart.

If you want to study a passage in more depth, our [Bible study guides](/bible/) can help.

## Our Daily Bread and Daily Bible Reading

Many readers use Our Daily Bread to supplement their regular Bible reading, not replace it. A good rhythm is to read the Bible passage in the morning, read the devotional, and then return to the verses once more in the evening.
