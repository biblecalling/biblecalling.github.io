Hi, I'm Amelia. I write the daily devotional reflections on this site.

Amelia is a Christian writer and devotional author based in New York City. She graduated from New York University and writes Bible-based reflections, prayers, and encouragement for everyday life.

Why I started writing

I write these reflections as a way to slow down with Scripture, think about the day's devotional reading, and share an honest reflection that may encourage someone else.

A little about me

I live in New York City, where I continue to write and study Scripture. I graduated from New York University, and my writing focuses on Bible-based reflections, prayer guides, and daily devotional encouragement for everyday faith.

How I write these posts

Every post starts with the day's reading in Sarah Young's Jesus Calling, such as our comprehensive month-long series in the Jesus Calling October 2026 guide. I read it first, and then I open my Bible and look at the verses behind the idea. What I write is my own reflection, in my own words.

I quote Scripture from the King James Version because it is in the public domain. I do not copy text from the book. If I'm not sure about something, I leave it out instead of guessing.

What I am not

I'm not presenting myself as a pastor, theologian, or counselor.

These posts are meant to encourage you, not to treat or diagnose anything. If you are dealing with anxiety, depression, grief, or anything that feels too heavy, please talk to a doctor, counselor, or pastor you trust. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency services or an appropriate crisis line.

About this website and Jesus Calling

This is an independent website. It is not connected to Sarah Young, her publisher, or the official Jesus Calling brand.

Jesus Calling is a devotional written from the point of view of Jesus. It is not Scripture. I read it as a prompt for reflection, and I always try to check ideas against the Bible. I'd encourage you to do the same.

Get in touch

If you have a question, a prayer request, or you've spotted a mistake, you can get in touch through the Contact page.
