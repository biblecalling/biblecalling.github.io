Jesus Calling Editorial Team

We are an independent team of Christian writers and editors devoted to publishing Bible-based encouragement, quiet-time reflections, daily prayers, and Scripture studies to help readers walk closely with the Lord every day.

Purpose of the Jesus Calling Website

The purpose of {{ site.title }} is to provide a quiet, Christ-centered digital haven for believers, seekers, and anyone carrying a heavy burden. In the midst of daily pressures, anxiety, and fast-paced distractions, we create a space where readers can pause, meditate on God's promises, and find genuine comfort through Scripture.

Our mission is rooted in Jesus' invitation in Matthew 11:28: “Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.” Everything we publish exists to draw your heart toward the peace, grace, and steady presence of Jesus Christ.

What Our Team Publishes

The Jesus Calling Editorial Team produces original, faith-building content designed to strengthen your daily spiritual walk:

  • Daily Devotionals: Thoughtful morning and evening reflections that help you start and end your day grounded in God's peace. Explore our daily devotional guides and our comprehensive month-long series in the Jesus Calling October 2026 guide.
  • Bible-Based Prayers: Heartfelt prayers for times of worry, uncertainty, illness, and gratitude. Visit our Christian prayer resources for personal and family prayer guides.
  • Scripture Collections & Commentary: Curated topical Bible verses addressing real-life struggles—including grief, patience, fear, and restoration—available in our Scripture study collection.
  • Encouragement for Daily Faith: Practical biblical guidance to help believers apply timeless spiritual truths to family life, work, relationships, and decision-making.

Our Approach to Scripture

We hold a profound respect for the inspired Word of God and take great care in how we handle biblical texts:

  • Careful Biblical Context: We do not treat verses as isolated slogans. We examine passages within their historical and textual contexts so readers understand what God's Word truly communicates.
  • Public Domain Translations: We quote Scripture primarily from the King James Version (KJV) and established public-domain biblical translations, ensuring clear, lawful, and accessible text.
  • Accessible, Clear Language: We explain biblical doctrines and devotional insights in plain, welcoming language without unnecessary academic jargon, so that every reader—from new seekers to longtime believers—can easily engage with Scripture.
  • Christ-Centered Emphasis: We consistently point readers to Jesus Christ as Lord and Redeemer, emphasizing grace, redemption, and a living relationship with God.

Our Editorial Review Process

To maintain high editorial integrity, our content follows a consistent review workflow:

  • Scripture Cross-Referencing: Before publication, every article is checked against biblical texts to verify that book, chapter, and verse references are accurate and that quotations match the cited translations.
  • Theological Consistency: Submissions are reviewed to confirm they reflect orthodox, historic Christian faith, avoiding speculative theological claims or out-of-context applications.
  • Original Writing & Clarity: We publish only original reflections, prayers, and studies written in our own words. We do not copy text from commercial books.
  • Independence & Pastoral Sensitivity: This is an independent Christian publication. We are not officially affiliated with Sarah Young, her estate, HarperCollins Christian Publishing, Thomas Nelson, Google, or any specific denomination. Furthermore, our writings provide spiritual encouragement and are not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or clinical care.
