Welcome to our Jesus Calling October 2026 daily devotional guide. Throughout this month, we explore key spiritual themes inspired by Sarah Young's beloved devotional, anchoring each day's reflection firmly in the Holy Scriptures, providing thoughtful commentary, practical applications, and original prayers.

October Reflection Anchor
“Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.”
— Proverbs 3:5–6 (KJV)
Editorial Clarity: Jesus Calling is a contemporary Christian devotional written by Sarah Young. The reflections published on this website are original commentary and study guides written by the independent Jesus Calling Editorial Team. Devotional readings serve as encouragement, whereas the Holy Bible stands as inspired, authoritative Scripture.
