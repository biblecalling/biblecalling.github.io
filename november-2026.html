Welcome to our Jesus Calling November 2026 companion reflection guide. Throughout this month, we explore deeply personal and spiritual themes—from finding peace amidst seasonal hurry and grief, to resting in God's sovereign care, cultivating gratitude, and pursuing genuine Christian fellowship. Each daily reflection is anchored in Scripture-based biblical encouragement, offering practical applications for your daily quiet time, and original prayers for peace and trust.

November Reflection Anchor
“Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.”
— Isaiah 26:3 (KJV)
Companion Reflection Notice: This collection contains independently written companion reflections. It is not the original text of, or a summary of, Jesus Calling by Sarah Young. Please consult your own copy of the book for its original daily readings. These reflections are written by the Bible Calling Editorial Team to encourage personal Scripture reading; the Holy Bible is the source of the Scripture passages quoted here.
