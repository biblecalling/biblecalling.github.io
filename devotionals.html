How to Start a Daily Devotional

Many people give up because they try to do too much. Start small and keep it steady.

1. Choose a time and place

Pick a time you can protect, such as before the phone, before the news, or after the kids leave for school. A fixed spot (a chair, a corner of the table) helps your mind settle faster.

2. Read slowly

Read the Bible passage once, then read it again. Notice a word or phrase that stands out. A devotional reading is not about finishing a chapter. It is about letting one thought sink in.

3. Ask two simple questions

  • What does this tell me about God?
  • What is one thing I can do or trust today because of it?

4. Pray in your own words

Talk to God about the day ahead: your work, your family, your worries. If words are hard to find, use one of our daily prayer guides.

5. Write one line

Keep a small notebook and write one sentence about what you noticed. After a month, reading those lines back is encouraging.

Types of Daily Devotionals

Not every devotional fits every person. Here are the main kinds.

Short scripture-and-reflection devotionals

One verse, a few paragraphs, and a prayer. These suit busy mornings and are the most common style.

Devotionals built around one Bible book

These follow a book such as Psalms or the Gospel of John, a few verses at a time. They suit people who want to understand context, not just a single verse.

Devotionals for a specific group

Some are written with certain readers in mind, such as women, couples or children, and speak to the situations those readers face.

Classic devotional books

Well-known devotional books have been used by readers for years, including Jesus Calling, My Utmost for His Highest, Our Daily Bread and The Upper Room. Each has a different tone, from quiet and personal to more teaching-focused.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a daily devotional?

A daily devotional is a short, regular time of Bible reading, reflection and prayer. Many devotionals pair a verse with a brief message to help you apply it to your day.

How long should a devotional take?

Most take between 5 and 15 minutes. A few minutes you keep every day will help you more than an hour you only manage once a month.

What is the best time of day for a devotional?

The best time is the one you will actually keep. Many people prefer the morning because it sets the tone for the day, but evenings work well too, especially for those with busy mornings.

Do I need a devotional book, or can I just read the Bible?

You do not need a book. Reading a Bible passage and praying is a devotional in itself. A devotional book or website simply gives you a guide and a daily thought to start from.

What if I miss a day?

Just start again the next day. A devotional habit is a relationship with God, not a streak to protect.
