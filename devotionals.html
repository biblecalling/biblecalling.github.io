Creating a Sustainable Quiet Time

A meaningful quiet time does not require hours of complex study or rigid perfection. It is an intentional invitation for Jesus to enter your day. Here is a simple, three-step rhythm that many Christians find life-giving:

  1. Pause and Breathe (2 minutes): Put away digital distractions. Take a few deep breaths, remembering that you are in the presence of the God who loves you.
  2. Read and Meditate (5–10 minutes): Read a short passage from our Bible encouragement guide or a daily devotional article, such as our Jesus Calling October reflections. Ask yourself: What does this teach me about God's character? How does this speak to my heart today?
  3. Pray and Surrender (3–5 minutes): Speak openly with the Lord about your schedule, fears, and hopes—using our daily prayer guides whenever words feel difficult to find. Entrust the day into His capable hands.
